Architecture Advanced Topics

Learners are expected to select one or two tracks aligned with their specific design, technical, or professional goals — completing every track is not required.

  • Computational Design and Digital Fabrication — Algorithmic form-finding, shape grammars, parametric logic, and robotic manufacturing processes.
  • Sustainable and High-Performance Building — Net-zero energy design, mass timber construction, circular material loops, and schematic decarbonization.
  • Urban Design and Housing — City-scale spatial analysis, urban morphology, collective housing typologies, and public realm design.
  • Historic Preservation and Adaptive Reuse — Conservation philosophy, federal rehabilitation standards, building pathology, and architectural conversion strategies.
  • Advanced Building Envelope and Structural Systems — High-rise lateral systems, long-span structural morphology, facade engineering, and exterior detailing.

Computational Design and Digital Fabrication

Computational methods, parametric modeling systems, and digital manufacturing technologies that transform generative design into materialized architecture.

Shape Grammars and Generative Theory: Introduction to Shape Grammars I (MIT OpenCourseWare) - Teaches visual computation and rule-based formal generation, establishing how geometric rules drive emergent design systems.

Parametric Modeling and Associative Logic: Elements of Parametric Design (Robert Woodbury) - Establishes the core thinking patterns, data flow dependencies, and geometric controllers required to build complex parametric models.

Digital Fabrication and Prototyping: Digital Design Fabrication (MIT OpenCourseWare) - Connects digital geometric models directly to computer-controlled production tools including CNC milling, 3D printing, and laser cutting.

Robotic Construction and Digital Materiality: The Robotic Touch: How Robots Change Architecture (Fabio Gramazio, Matthias Kohler, and Jan Willmann) - Documents advanced research in multi-axis robotic fabrication, additive material placement, and the architectural tectonics of automated construction.

Sustainable and High-Performance Building

Advanced methodologies for designing carbon-neutral, energy-positive, and resource-circular architecture across full building lifecycles.

Net-Zero Energy Climate Concepts: Zero-Energy Design: An Approach to Make Your Building Sustainable (TU Delft OpenCourseWare) - Teaches a stepped approach to achieving net-zero energy performance through climate analysis, passive envelope optimization, and active system integration.

Mass Timber and Carbon Sequestration: Sustainable Building with Timber (DelftX via edX) - Covers modern engineered timber systems, life-cycle carbon accounting, prefabricated assemblies, and structural joinery for tall timber buildings.

Circular Economy and Material Loops: Circular Economy for a Sustainable Built Environment (TU Delft OpenCourseWare) - Focuses on closed-loop resource management, design for disassembly, material passports, and circularity across materials, buildings, and urban regions.

Schematic Decarbonization and System Sizing: The Green Studio Handbook: Environmental Strategies for Schematic Design (Alison G. Kwok and Walter T. Grondzik) - Provides practical rules of thumb, sizing calculations, and integration protocols for over 40 active and passive environmental technologies during early-stage design.

Urban Design and Housing

Spatial analysis, urban form development, and inclusive housing typologies addressing social and environmental density.

Urban Development and Policy: Introduction to Urban Design and Development (MIT OpenCourseWare) - Analyzes the economic, spatial, and institutional forces shaping cities through case studies in public infrastructure and large-scale redevelopment.

Urban Perception and Cognitive Mapping: The Image of the City (Kevin Lynch) - Establishes the foundational theory of urban legibility and cognitive mapping, categorizing the built environment into paths, edges, districts, nodes, and landmarks.

Urban Field Methods and Morphological Analysis: Urban Design Skills: Observing, Interpreting, and Representing the City (MIT OpenCourseWare) - Instructs learners in fieldwork methods, street profile sectioning, block typology analysis, and urban infill design.

Collective and Inclusive Housing Typologies: Global Housing Design (DelftX via edX) - Investigates spatial clustering, incremental expansion strategies, and high-density residential design across diverse global socioeconomic contexts.

Historic Preservation and Adaptive Reuse

Methodologies, conservation philosophies, and technical standards for documenting, preserving, and repurposing existing structures.

Preservation Standards and Regulatory Compliance: Technical Preservation Services Training (U.S. National Park Service) - Comprehensive webinar modules on applying the Secretary of the Interior's Standards for Rehabilitation, substitute materials, and building code variances for historic structures.

Preservation Theory and Documentation: Historic Preservation: An Introduction to Its History, Principles, and Practice (Norman Tyler, Ted J. Ligibel, and Ilene R. Tyler) - Surveys preservation philosophies, landmark designations, historic district zoning, preservation technology, and material pathology.

Building Adaptation Feasibility: Managing Building Adaptation: A Sustainable Approach (TU Delft OpenCourseWare) - Delivers analytical tools to assess the structural, functional, and financial viability of transforming obsolete buildings into sustainable assets.

Typological Strategies of Adaptive Reuse: Adaptive Reuse in Architecture: A Typological Index (Liliane Wong) - Categorizes contemporary intervention strategies across 50 global case studies, formulating design frameworks for structural insertions within existing fabric.

Advanced Building Envelope and Structural Systems

The technical integration of complex lateral systems, long-span structural typologies, and high-performance building enclosures.

Structural Morphology and Typological Classification: Structure Systems (Heino Engel) - A comprehensive graphic taxonomy categorizing structural behavior into form-active, vector-active, section-active, and surface-active systems.

High-Rise and Long-Span Systems: Building Technology III: Building Structural Systems (MIT OpenCourseWare) - Examines indeterminate structural behavior, shear walls, outrigger frameworks, and structural integration in high-rise and large-span buildings.

Façade Engineering Principles: Façade Design and Engineering: Complexity Made Simple (DelftX via edX) - Details the technical mechanics of building enclosures, addressing curtain wall systems, thermal bridges, tolerances, and weatherproofing barriers.

Envelope Detailing and Assembly Systems: Modern Construction Envelopes (Andrew Watts) - An exhaustive graphic reference manual covering the detailing, joint design, structural support, and material interfaces of modern glass, metal, masonry, and composite facades.

# Architecture Advanced Topics

Learners are expected to select one or two tracks aligned with their specific design, technical, or professional goals — completing every track is not required.

- **Computational Design and Digital Fabrication** — Algorithmic form-finding, shape grammars, parametric logic, and robotic manufacturing processes.
- **Sustainable and High-Performance Building** — Net-zero energy design, mass timber construction, circular material loops, and schematic decarbonization.
- **Urban Design and Housing** — City-scale spatial analysis, urban morphology, collective housing typologies, and public realm design.
- **Historic Preservation and Adaptive Reuse** — Conservation philosophy, federal rehabilitation standards, building pathology, and architectural conversion strategies.
- **Advanced Building Envelope and Structural Systems** — High-rise lateral systems, long-span structural morphology, facade engineering, and exterior detailing.

## Computational Design and Digital Fabrication

Computational methods, parametric modeling systems, and digital manufacturing technologies that transform generative design into materialized architecture.

Shape Grammars and Generative Theory: [Introduction to Shape Grammars I (MIT OpenCourseWare)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/4-540-introduction-to-shape-grammars-i-fall-2018) - Teaches visual computation and rule-based formal generation, establishing how geometric rules drive emergent design systems.

Parametric Modeling and Associative Logic: [Elements of Parametric Design (Robert Woodbury)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780415779876) - Establishes the core thinking patterns, data flow dependencies, and geometric controllers required to build complex parametric models.

Digital Fabrication and Prototyping: [Digital Design Fabrication (MIT OpenCourseWare)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/4-510-digital-design-fabrication-fall-2008) - Connects digital geometric models directly to computer-controlled production tools including CNC milling, 3D printing, and laser cutting.

Robotic Construction and Digital Materiality: [The Robotic Touch: How Robots Change Architecture (Fabio Gramazio, Matthias Kohler, and Jan Willmann)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9783906027371) - Documents advanced research in multi-axis robotic fabrication, additive material placement, and the architectural tectonics of automated construction.

## Sustainable and High-Performance Building

Advanced methodologies for designing carbon-neutral, energy-positive, and resource-circular architecture across full building lifecycles.

Net-Zero Energy Climate Concepts: [Zero-Energy Design: An Approach to Make Your Building Sustainable (TU Delft OpenCourseWare)](https://ocw.tudelft.nl/courses/zero-energy-design-an-approach-to-make-your-building-sustainable/) - Teaches a stepped approach to achieving net-zero energy performance through climate analysis, passive envelope optimization, and active system integration.

Mass Timber and Carbon Sequestration: [Sustainable Building with Timber (DelftX via edX)](https://www.edx.org/learn/architecture/delft-university-of-technology-building-with-timber) - Covers modern engineered timber systems, life-cycle carbon accounting, prefabricated assemblies, and structural joinery for tall timber buildings.

Circular Economy and Material Loops: [Circular Economy for a Sustainable Built Environment (TU Delft OpenCourseWare)](https://ocw.tudelft.nl/courses/circular-economy-for-a-sustainable-built-environment/) - Focuses on closed-loop resource management, design for disassembly, material passports, and circularity across materials, buildings, and urban regions.

Schematic Decarbonization and System Sizing: [The Green Studio Handbook: Environmental Strategies for Schematic Design (Alison G. Kwok and Walter T. Grondzik)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781032584362) - Provides practical rules of thumb, sizing calculations, and integration protocols for over 40 active and passive environmental technologies during early-stage design.

## Urban Design and Housing

Spatial analysis, urban form development, and inclusive housing typologies addressing social and environmental density.

Urban Development and Policy: [Introduction to Urban Design and Development (MIT OpenCourseWare)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/11-301j-introduction-to-urban-design-and-development-fall-2016) - Analyzes the economic, spatial, and institutional forces shaping cities through case studies in public infrastructure and large-scale redevelopment.

Urban Perception and Cognitive Mapping: [The Image of the City (Kevin Lynch)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780262620017) - Establishes the foundational theory of urban legibility and cognitive mapping, categorizing the built environment into paths, edges, districts, nodes, and landmarks.

Urban Field Methods and Morphological Analysis: [Urban Design Skills: Observing, Interpreting, and Representing the City (MIT OpenCourseWare)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/11-328j-urban-design-skills-observing-interpreting-and-representing-the-city-fall-2004) - Instructs learners in fieldwork methods, street profile sectioning, block typology analysis, and urban infill design.

Collective and Inclusive Housing Typologies: [Global Housing Design (DelftX via edX)](https://www.edx.org/learn/housing/delft-university-of-technology-global-housing-design) - Investigates spatial clustering, incremental expansion strategies, and high-density residential design across diverse global socioeconomic contexts.

## Historic Preservation and Adaptive Reuse

Methodologies, conservation philosophies, and technical standards for documenting, preserving, and repurposing existing structures.

Preservation Standards and Regulatory Compliance: [Technical Preservation Services Training (U.S. National Park Service)](https://www.nps.gov/orgs/1739/training.htm) - Comprehensive webinar modules on applying the Secretary of the Interior's Standards for Rehabilitation, substitute materials, and building code variances for historic structures.

Preservation Theory and Documentation: [Historic Preservation: An Introduction to Its History, Principles, and Practice (Norman Tyler, Ted J. Ligibel, and Ilene R. Tyler)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780393712971) - Surveys preservation philosophies, landmark designations, historic district zoning, preservation technology, and material pathology.

Building Adaptation Feasibility: [Managing Building Adaptation: A Sustainable Approach (TU Delft OpenCourseWare)](https://ocw.tudelft.nl/courses/managing-building-adaptation-a-sustainable-approach/) - Delivers analytical tools to assess the structural, functional, and financial viability of transforming obsolete buildings into sustainable assets.

Typological Strategies of Adaptive Reuse: [Adaptive Reuse in Architecture: A Typological Index (Liliane Wong)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9783035625639) - Categorizes contemporary intervention strategies across 50 global case studies, formulating design frameworks for structural insertions within existing fabric.

## Advanced Building Envelope and Structural Systems

The technical integration of complex lateral systems, long-span structural typologies, and high-performance building enclosures.

Structural Morphology and Typological Classification: [Structure Systems (Heino Engel)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9783775718769) - A comprehensive graphic taxonomy categorizing structural behavior into form-active, vector-active, section-active, and surface-active systems.

High-Rise and Long-Span Systems: [Building Technology III: Building Structural Systems (MIT OpenCourseWare)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/4-463-building-technology-iii-building-structural-systems-fall-2004) - Examines indeterminate structural behavior, shear walls, outrigger frameworks, and structural integration in high-rise and large-span buildings.

Façade Engineering Principles: [Façade Design and Engineering: Complexity Made Simple (DelftX via edX)](https://www.edx.org/learn/engineering/delft-university-of-technology-facade-design-and-engineering-complexity-made-simple) - Details the technical mechanics of building enclosures, addressing curtain wall systems, thermal bridges, tolerances, and weatherproofing barriers.

Envelope Detailing and Assembly Systems: [Modern Construction Envelopes (Andrew Watts)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9783035617702) - An exhaustive graphic reference manual covering the detailing, joint design, structural support, and material interfaces of modern glass, metal, masonry, and composite facades.

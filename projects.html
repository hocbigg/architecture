Conduct your own architectural precedent studies

  • Architecture Precedent Study and Analysis (First In Architecture) - A structured guide explaining how to deconstruct existing buildings, extract spatial and tectonic principles, and diagram geometric relationships.
  • How to Analyse Architecture Case Studies as a Beginner ((to)scale) - A beginner-oriented walkthrough covering desktop research, project deconstruction, and translating lessons into your own design drivers.
  • Architecture Precedent Study Guide (ArchiSoup) - A comprehensive methodology for selecting relevant architectural case studies, analyzing program and circulation, and translating observations into visual diagrams.

Produce your own architectural site analyses

  • Architecture Site Analysis Guide: Data Collection to Presentation (First In Architecture) - A step-by-step walkthrough covering desktop research, on-site fieldwork, sun-path mapping, prevailing winds, and graphic synthesis.
  • Guide to Site Analysis (Monograph) - A professional-grade guide detailing the research, analysis, and synthesis phases of site context, zoning envelopes, and physical constraints.
  • Architecture Site Analysis Guide: Techniques, Tools, and Tips (ArchiSoup) - Search "Architecture Site Analysis Guide: Techniques, Tools, and Tips" on archisoup.com. A practical methodology for documenting environmental forces, regulatory boundaries, and site diagrams.

Draft your own architectural floor plans

  • Technical Drawing Plans (First In Architecture) - A comprehensive guide covering standard floor plan drawing conventions, cut planes, wall thicknesses, door swings, annotations, and graphic symbols.
  • Architectural Floor Plan Checklist (First In Architecture) - A rigorous quality-control checklist and guide to verify room dimensions, structural openings, circulation paths, and drawing notations.
  • Space Planning Basics: Introduction for Architectural Design (First In Architecture) - A tutorial on translating client programmatic requirements into bubble diagrams, adjacency matrices, and resolved spatial layouts.
  • Sketch to Finished Floor Plan: My Process, Graphics and Settings (30X40 Design Workshop) - Search "Sketch to Finished Floor Plan 30X40" on YouTube. An architect's video walkthrough moving from trace-paper concept sketches to a fully coordinated CAD floor plan.

Build your own architectural scale models

  • Architectural Model Making Tips (30X40 Design Workshop) - A practitioner's manual covering material selection (basswood, chipboard, museum board), essential tools, cutting jigs, and clean assembly techniques.
  • Architectural Model Making: Materials, Tools and Techniques (How to Rhino) - A guide detailing scale selection, toolkits, and step-by-step assembly methods for study and presentation models.
  • Architectural Model Making Techniques and Tutorial (30X40 Design Workshop) - Search "Architectural Model Making Techniques and Tutorial 30X40" on YouTube. A step-by-step physical model build showing how to construct a clean study model from scratch.

Draft your own architectural construction details

  • Technical Drawing: Construction Drawings (First In Architecture) - A guide to assembling technical construction drawing packages, showing how assemblies, materials, and structural interfaces are documented.
  • Architectural Graphics 101: Drawing Alignment and Notes (Life of an Architect) - A drafting guide on detail sheet composition, dimension placement, leader-line discipline, and drawing note alignment.
  • Architectural Graphic Standards: Part 2 (Life of an Architect) - A detailed breakdown of large-scale (3" = 1'-0") detailing conventions, material hatching standards, and flashing assemblies.
  • How to Get Better at Architectural Detailing (First In Architecture) - A methodological guide on analyzing job-site construction, tracking moisture barriers, and drafting continuous thermal envelopes.

Draw your own architectural perspective sketches

  • Architectural Sketching or How to Sketch Like Me (Life of an Architect) - A practitioner's tutorial by architect Bob Borson on linework hierarchy, pencil techniques, and iterative trace-paper sketching.
  • My Approach to Sketching Architecture (Liz Steel) - A structured method for drawing buildings by breaking down complex architecture into primary volumes, structural frameworks, depths, and shadows.
  • 7-Step Perspective Drawing: Architecture Design Concept Development (Sketch Like an Architect) - Search "7-Step Perspective Drawing Sketch Like an Architect" on sketchlikeanarchitect.com. A tutorial demonstrating how to construct a 2-point perspective grid, mass a building concept, and render depth with shadows.

Assemble your own architecture portfolios

  • The Definitive Guide to an Epic Architecture Portfolio: Part I (Young Architect) - A foundational guide on identifying portfolio goals, defining your audience, and building a compelling personal design narrative.
  • The Definitive Guide to an Epic Architecture Portfolio: Part II (Young Architect) - A detailed guide covering content strategy, project selection, visual hierarchy, and composition grids.
  • The Definitive Guide to an Epic Architecture Portfolio: Part III (Young Architect) - A production walkthrough detailing formatting choices, print versus digital publishing, software workflows, and pre-flight checks.

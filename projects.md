## Conduct your own architectural precedent studies

- [Architecture Precedent Study and Analysis (First In Architecture)](https://www.firstinarchitecture.co.uk/architecture-precedent-study-and-analysis/) - A structured guide explaining how to deconstruct existing buildings, extract spatial and tectonic principles, and diagram geometric relationships.
- [How to Analyse Architecture Case Studies as a Beginner ((to)scale)](https://www.toscaleblog.co.uk/blog/analysing-architecture-case-studies-for-beginners) - A beginner-oriented walkthrough covering desktop research, project deconstruction, and translating lessons into your own design drivers.
- [Architecture Precedent Study Guide (ArchiSoup)](https://www.archisoup.com/studio-guide/precedent-study-guide) - A comprehensive methodology for selecting relevant architectural case studies, analyzing program and circulation, and translating observations into visual diagrams.

## Produce your own architectural site analyses

- [Architecture Site Analysis Guide: Data Collection to Presentation (First In Architecture)](https://www.firstinarchitecture.co.uk/architecture-site-analysis-guide/) - A step-by-step walkthrough covering desktop research, on-site fieldwork, sun-path mapping, prevailing winds, and graphic synthesis.
- [Guide to Site Analysis (Monograph)](https://monograph.com/blog/site-analysis-architecture) - A professional-grade guide detailing the research, analysis, and synthesis phases of site context, zoning envelopes, and physical constraints.
- Architecture Site Analysis Guide: Techniques, Tools, and Tips (ArchiSoup) - Search "Architecture Site Analysis Guide: Techniques, Tools, and Tips" on archisoup.com. A practical methodology for documenting environmental forces, regulatory boundaries, and site diagrams.

## Draft your own architectural floor plans

- [Technical Drawing Plans (First In Architecture)](https://www.firstinarchitecture.co.uk/technical-drawing-plans/) - A comprehensive guide covering standard floor plan drawing conventions, cut planes, wall thicknesses, door swings, annotations, and graphic symbols.
- [Architectural Floor Plan Checklist (First In Architecture)](https://www.firstinarchitecture.co.uk/architectural-floor-plan-checklist/) - A rigorous quality-control checklist and guide to verify room dimensions, structural openings, circulation paths, and drawing notations.
- [Space Planning Basics: Introduction for Architectural Design (First In Architecture)](https://www.firstinarchitecture.co.uk/space-planning-basics/) - A tutorial on translating client programmatic requirements into bubble diagrams, adjacency matrices, and resolved spatial layouts.
- Sketch to Finished Floor Plan: My Process, Graphics and Settings (30X40 Design Workshop) - Search "Sketch to Finished Floor Plan 30X40" on YouTube. An architect's video walkthrough moving from trace-paper concept sketches to a fully coordinated CAD floor plan.

## Build your own architectural scale models

- [Architectural Model Making Tips (30X40 Design Workshop)](https://thirtybyforty.com/blog/architectural-model-making-tips) - A practitioner's manual covering material selection (basswood, chipboard, museum board), essential tools, cutting jigs, and clean assembly techniques.
- [Architectural Model Making: Materials, Tools and Techniques (How to Rhino)](https://howtorhino.com/blog/architecture-education/architectural-model-making) - A guide detailing scale selection, toolkits, and step-by-step assembly methods for study and presentation models.
- Architectural Model Making Techniques and Tutorial (30X40 Design Workshop) - Search "Architectural Model Making Techniques and Tutorial 30X40" on YouTube. A step-by-step physical model build showing how to construct a clean study model from scratch.

## Draft your own architectural construction details

- [Technical Drawing: Construction Drawings (First In Architecture)](https://www.firstinarchitecture.co.uk/technical-drawing-construction-drawings/) - A guide to assembling technical construction drawing packages, showing how assemblies, materials, and structural interfaces are documented.
- [Architectural Graphics 101: Drawing Alignment and Notes (Life of an Architect)](https://www.lifeofanarchitect.com/architectural-graphics-101-drawing-alignment-and-notes/) - A drafting guide on detail sheet composition, dimension placement, leader-line discipline, and drawing note alignment.
- [Architectural Graphic Standards: Part 2 (Life of an Architect)](https://www.lifeofanarchitect.com/architectural-graphic-standards-part-2/) - A detailed breakdown of large-scale (3" = 1'-0") detailing conventions, material hatching standards, and flashing assemblies.
- [How to Get Better at Architectural Detailing (First In Architecture)](https://www.firstinarchitecture.co.uk/how-to-get-better-at-architectural-detailing/) - A methodological guide on analyzing job-site construction, tracking moisture barriers, and drafting continuous thermal envelopes.

## Draw your own architectural perspective sketches

- [Architectural Sketching or How to Sketch Like Me (Life of an Architect)](https://www.lifeofanarchitect.com/architectural-sketching-or-how-to-sketch-like-me/) - A practitioner's tutorial by architect Bob Borson on linework hierarchy, pencil techniques, and iterative trace-paper sketching.
- [My Approach to Sketching Architecture (Liz Steel)](https://www.lizsteel.com/my-approach-to-sketching-architecture/) - A structured method for drawing buildings by breaking down complex architecture into primary volumes, structural frameworks, depths, and shadows.
- 7-Step Perspective Drawing: Architecture Design Concept Development (Sketch Like an Architect) - Search "7-Step Perspective Drawing Sketch Like an Architect" on sketchlikeanarchitect.com. A tutorial demonstrating how to construct a 2-point perspective grid, mass a building concept, and render depth with shadows.

- [The Definitive Guide to an Epic Architecture Portfolio: Part II (Young Architect)](https://youngarchitect.com/the-definitive-guide-to-an-epic-architecture-portfolio-part-ii) - A detailed guide covering content strategy, project selection, visual hierarchy, and composition grids.
- [The Definitive Guide to an Epic Architecture Portfolio: Part III (Young Architect)](https://youngarchitect.com/the-definitive-guide-to-an-epic-architecture-portfolio-part-iii) - A production walkthrough detailing formatting choices, print versus digital publishing, software workflows, and pre-flight checks.
